How to read the scores. Scores are ordinal decision aids, not truth. Value band, pathway status (GREEN/AMBER/RED) and evidence confidence are three separate judgements and are never combined into a single verdict. Raw weighted scores are the headline throughout; renormalised values appear only as declared sensitivity views. Criterion weights and band boundaries remain provisional pending governance approval. Analysis is not authority — partner-intent declarations, release decisions and commitments rest with authorised humans.
Built from publicly available sources and the country-agnostic V7 framework. No classified, caveated or partner-controlled information is used or should be added.
Every record is stamped custodian validation pending — nothing here is Defence-validated.
