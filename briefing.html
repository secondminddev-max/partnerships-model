Scoring (Framework V7)

Ten criteria, 0–5 against anchored scales; weighted points = weight × score/5; headline = raw weighted sum on the full 100-point set. Weights: priority alignment 20; capability maturity, mutual benefit, value proposition 15 each; instrument/forum 10; security, technical, policy, time/resources, sovereignty 5 each. Unknown criteria contribute no points. Partner scores exist only per opportunity — no universal best-partner ranking (V7 §13 hard rule).
